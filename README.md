# Baya3-Komisyoung
A B2B2C E-commerce Platform for Egypt

Connecting sellers, agents, and consumers with a verified owner system (MedhatCIL - Consumer In Loop).
By Medhat Abbas.

## What is it?
A next-generation platform connecting three parties:
- Baya3 (Seller/Wholesaler)
- Komisyoungy (Wholesaler/Factory)  
- Consumer in the Loop (CIL) - The decision maker

## Vision
To create a trusted local marketplace where the street vendor (trader) connects directly with buyer (by phone or WhatsApp). All responsibility protected.

- Commission zero from Baya3 only
- Handling with Komisyoungy as quantity discount and marketing in Baya3
